Working the land, working the forge

I'm Martim. I keep a small farm at Póvoa de Baixo, in the parish of Beduído, Estarreja, and split my week between the fields and the forge behind the barn.

This site is the logbook: what got planted or sprayed, how much rain fell, which birds turned up, and what came off the anvil. Most of it is written down here before it's written down anywhere else.
