Finished a double gate this week, commissioned by a neighbour up the road. Mild steel, hand-forged hinges, a simple lattice pattern rather than anything ornamental — it has to survive goats, not a garden show.

## Notes from the build

1. Cut and squared the frame stock first, before any of the decorative work
2. Forged the hinge straps last, once the frame was tacked and checked for square
3. Two coats of a matte black finish, left to cure a full week before hanging

The trick with field gates is restraint. Anything too delicate gets bent within a season.
