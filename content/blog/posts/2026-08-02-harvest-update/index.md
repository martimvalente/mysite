The lower field came in this week. Yield is down slightly on last year but the grain is dry enough that it went straight into storage without a second drying pass.

## What got done

- Cut and baled the lower field on Tuesday and Wednesday
- Moved the last of the straw up to the barn before the Thursday rain
- Checked the irrigation lines in the orchard — one dripper needs replacing

> The old rule still holds: cut on a dry morning, bale before the dew comes back.

Next week is machinery maintenance — the tractor is due an oil change before it goes back out for the second cut.
