---
title: First frost of the season
date: 2025-11-20
category: Field Notes
---

Woke up to a white field this morning — the first proper frost of the year, about three weeks later than usual. Covered the last of the tender herbs the night before, more from habit than expectation, and it paid off.

The birds have noticed the change too; fewer swallows about this week, more of the winter regulars settling in around the hedge line.
